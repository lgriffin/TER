How to read these charts

Natural limits
What the process does on its own: the mean of the baseline sessions plus or minus 2.66 average moving ranges (3.145 median moving ranges). Sessions inside them are routine variation.
Tuned limits
Action limits a developer set, with a reason. They replace the natural limits for the beyond-limits rule only.
Beyond limits
One session outside a control limit.
Two of three
Two of three successive sessions more than two sigma from the centre line, on the same side.
Four of five
Four of five successive sessions more than one sigma from the centre line, on the same side.
Run of eight
Eight successive sessions on the same side of the centre line.
Fires
An unfavourable signal on a ratio or count measure: the kind an advisory policy may act on at L4. Token and time totals never fire.
